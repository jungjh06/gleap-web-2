[ ABOUT GLEAP ]

Empowering Scientific Leadership

GLEAP (Global Leadership Program) is the official student honors organization of the College of Natural Sciences at Seoul National University. Founded with the mission to bridge the gap between rigorous scientific research and broader societal impact, we cultivate leaders who can communicate complex ideas and drive innovation.

Our members are selected from the brightest minds across the college, engaging in interdisciplinary projects, academic seminars, and extensive community outreach programs. We believe that true scientific advancement requires not just lab work, but collaboration, ethical grounding, and global perspective.
